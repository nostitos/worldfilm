# BRIEF — __PROJECT_SLUG__

Fill every line; "unknown" is allowed and the director decides. Read first by the worldfilm director (stage 0).

## The film
- Format: length, aspect, fps (60 for motion graphics and camera work, 30 for talking-head, 24 for film cadence)
- Extra sizes: none | 9:16 vertical | 1:1 — each extra size is its own film at the gates (own render, own pops / luma /
  run_gates, own PROGRESS.md row); say which moments must survive the crop
- Deadline and what "done" means (master MP4 + poster + SRT + music-only version are standard)

## The world
- What is simulated: a city of data, a swarm, a machine, a chart as terrain, a real room (footage)
- The one constant (what never changes) and the one variable (what the film is about)
- Data: files in data/, with the source of every number (goes into facts.json; nothing else reaches the screen)
- Footage: file in footage/, what the subject is, what may sit behind the subject

## The camera (set once; a branding decision)
- Camera dial 1-5: 1 Locked, 2 Anchored, 3 Motivated (default), 4 Flowing, 5 Perpetual
- Signature moves allowed to repeat (push, orbit, crane, dolly, rack)
- Designed stops (where the camera rests on purpose, e.g. "NOW"): each one is a boosted rest drift, never a still frame
- Framing targets (what must sit where on screen at a given moment: "the horizon in the top fifth at the end")

## Look
- Motion personality: one word (calm, precise, urgent, playful, monumental) and three materials ("concrete, brass, fog")
- References: 2-6 videos or stills in references/ (measured, never copied); which one is the benchmark
- Palette / type if they exist (hex, font files); otherwise derived from the measured reference

## Sound
- Voiceover: none | lines in narration.txt (recorded first; the picture retimes to the voice)
- Music: original synthesized score | a track in assets/music/ (rights holder, where it may be published)
- The silence: where the film goes quiet before the payoff (one window, exact seconds; it becomes digital zeros)
- Who will listen before it ships (name a person; the gates measure, they do not hear)

## Facts and rights
- facts.json holds every number, name, date and claim that may appear on screen
- Image/footage rights: yours to use? Generated imagery is allowed only as a labelled concept layer

## Must / must not
- Three signature moments the viewer should remember
- Must-nots (no faces, no brand names, no flashing, no jokes)
- One thing you would be unhappy about if it went wrong
