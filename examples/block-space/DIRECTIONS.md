# DIRECTIONS — three looks, two killed (worldfilm stage 2)

## A. Paper and pencil (killed)
The schedule drawn as the "21,000,000" film drew it: a pencil line on cream paper, the halvings as steps. Honest, in the
house style, and already made. Killed because it is a chart, and the brief asks for a place: nothing in it needs a
camera, so the camera dial would be a lie.

## B. Neon grid (killed)
A synthwave grid of glowing bars racing toward a sun, numbers in neon. It moves well and reads at a glance on a phone.
Killed because the look is borrowed from a thousand crypto reels, because glow hides the honesty of a measured height,
and because the brief says serious: the viewer should feel the weight of the rule, not a vibe.

## C. The concrete boulevard (chosen)
A night boulevard of concrete slabs, one per difficulty period, height = subsidy. The past is solid, the scheduled
future is wireframe, the halvings are orange gates. One camera flies the whole chain: down the canyon of the first
epochs, stopping at NOW, then craning up as the slabs thin to hairlines and the street ends at block 6,929,999. The
look costs nothing to believe: a slab is a slab, a gate is a gate, and the only colour is the one that marks the rule.
