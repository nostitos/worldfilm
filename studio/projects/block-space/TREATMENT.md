# TREATMENT — Block Space (30 s)

## Logline
The issuance schedule of Bitcoin, built as a city and flown through once: the past is concrete, the future is already
drawn, and the street ends.

## Three candidate structures
1. Chronological fly-through, past to future, ending on the cap (chosen: the rule is a sequence, so the film is one).
2. Reverse: start at 2140 and fly back to the genesis block (rejected: the payoff is the end of issuance, not the start).
3. Two-screen split past/future (rejected: a split frame reads as a chart; the brief asks for a place).

## Benchmark
Learn from the reference engine sim: a world built once, a camera that flies through it, labels pinned to parts.
Do not learn its colour or its cheer. Learn from "21,000,000": every number sourced, one accent, a silence before the
payoff. Do not learn its paper.

## Cue map
Picture events (film/data.js events) drive every sound: halving-1..4 -> kicks; halving-5..32 -> ticks; now -> blip;
end -> riser; silence 25.5-27.5; cap -> impact. Whooshes sit on the three camera velocity peaks.

## Subtitle design
Captions: Newsreader 40 px, bottom-left, 1,200 px wide. Labels: IBM Plex Mono 22 px, orange left rule, pinned to the
gate they describe, pushed up when two would collide. Corner readout: BLOCK / SUBSIDY / MINED or SCHEDULED, top-right.
